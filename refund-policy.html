Cancellation & Refund Policy

Plans change, especially with international flights. This policy explains how deposits, cancellations, changes and refunds work for every Ceylon Chauffeurs booking.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Cancellation & Refund Terms & Conditions Privacy Policy

1. Booking deposit

Your booking is confirmed once we receive a 20% advance deposit of the total quoted price. The balance is paid in cash (USD, EUR or LKR) to your chauffeur on the first day of your tour, or by bank transfer before your tour starts. For single airport transfers we may confirm without a deposit; the full fare is then paid to the chauffeur.

All prices are quoted in US dollars. If you pay in another currency, the exchange rate on the day of payment applies.

2. Cancellation by you

Cancellation time is counted from the confirmed pickup time on the first day of your booking.

When you cancelWhat you get back
48 hours or more before pickup100% of your deposit, with no admin fee
Between 24 and 48 hours before pickup80% of your deposit, or move your dates free of charge within 12 months
Less than 24 hours before pickupThe deposit is non-refundable
No-show (you are not at the pickup point and we cannot reach you within 2 hours)The deposit is non-refundable

Please cancel in writing by email to bookings@ceylonchauffeur.com or by WhatsApp on +94 76 054 2557. We will confirm your cancellation in writing.

3. Hotels, safaris, tickets and other third-party bookings

If you ask us to book hotels, safari jeeps, train tickets, entrance tickets or activities for you, those bookings follow the cancellation rules of each hotel or supplier. We will refund whatever the supplier refunds to us and tell you before booking if anything is non-refundable.

4. Changing your booking

  • Flight delays: we track your flight into Colombo (CMB). If your flight is delayed, your chauffeur waits at no extra cost and there is no penalty.
  • Date changes: moving your dates 48 hours or more before pickup is free, subject to vehicle availability.
  • Route changes during the tour: reasonable detours, extra stops and changes to daily start times are free. Adding extra days or a much longer route is charged at the agreed daily rate.
  • Vehicle upgrades: if you add passengers and need a larger vehicle, the price difference applies.

5. Ending a tour early

If you decide to end your tour early for personal reasons, unused days are not refunded. If you have to end the tour because of a medical emergency, contact us and we will look at a partial refund or credit case by case.

6. Cancellation by Ceylon Chauffeurs

If we have to cancel your booking for any reason within our control, you receive a 100% refund of everything you have paid. If a vehicle breaks down during your tour, we send a replacement of the same or higher class as quickly as possible. If we cannot, the affected days are refunded in full.

7. Events outside anyone’s control

If travel becomes unsafe or impossible because of natural disasters, severe weather, floods, landslides, curfews, strikes, government travel restrictions or similar events, you may move your booking to new dates within 12 months at no cost, or receive a refund of your deposit minus any non-refundable third-party costs we have already paid on your behalf.

8. How and when refunds are paid

Refunds are paid to the same method you used to pay (bank transfer, card or online payment) within 7 to 14 working days after we confirm your cancellation. Bank charges and currency conversion fees charged by your bank or payment provider are not covered by us.

9. Questions

If something has gone wrong with your booking, please tell us. Email bookings@ceylonchauffeur.com or message us on WhatsApp, and we will reply within 24 hours.
