Cancellation & Refund Terms & Conditions Privacy Policy

1. About us

Ceylon Chauffeurs (ceylonchauffeur.com) provides private chauffeur-driven vehicles, airport transfers and tailor-made tours in Sri Lanka. In these terms, “we” and “us” means Ceylon Chauffeurs, and “you” means the person making the booking and everyone travelling in that booking.

2. Quotes and bookings

  • The rate estimate shown on the website is a guide. Your final price is the one in the written quotation we send you by email or WhatsApp.
  • A booking is confirmed only when we have received your deposit (see our Cancellation & Refund Policy) and sent you a written confirmation.
  • Please check your confirmation carefully (names, dates, pickup point, flight number and number of passengers) and tell us about any mistakes straight away.
  • The person making the booking must be at least 18 years old and accepts these terms for everyone in the group.

3. Prices and payment

  • Prices are quoted in US dollars per vehicle (not per person), unless stated otherwise.
  • A 20% deposit confirms your booking. The balance is paid in cash to your chauffeur on the first day of the tour or by bank transfer before the tour starts.
  • Cancellations, changes and refunds are covered by our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

4. What is included

Included in the daily rate: the vehicle, a licensed English-speaking chauffeur, fuel, unlimited mileage on your agreed route, expressway tolls, vehicle parking fees, and the chauffeur’s meals and accommodation.

Not included (unless your quotation says so): your hotels and meals, entrance tickets to sites (for example Sigiriya, Polonnaruwa, the Temple of the Tooth), national park and safari jeep fees, train tickets, activities, tips and personal expenses.

5. Driving hours and safety

  • For your safety, chauffeurs drive during daylight and reasonable hours. Long driving days are planned with rest stops. Night driving on long routes may be limited.
  • Your chauffeur may refuse to drive in a way, or on a road, they believe is unsafe, and may refuse to carry anyone who is aggressive or heavily intoxicated.
  • Seat belts must be worn where fitted. Child seats are available on request and must be requested when booking.
  • Vehicles are strictly non-smoking. Illegal items may not be carried in the vehicle.

6. Your responsibilities

  • Be at the agreed pickup point on time. If you are delayed, let your chauffeur or our team know on WhatsApp.
  • Keep your passports, money and valuables with you. We are not responsible for items left in the vehicle, but we will do our best to return anything found.
  • You are responsible for any damage to the vehicle caused deliberately or carelessly by your group, beyond normal wear (for example, spills that need professional cleaning).
  • Make sure you have a valid visa / ETA for Sri Lanka and suitable travel insurance, including medical cover.

7. Luggage and passengers

Each vehicle has a maximum number of passengers and a luggage limit, shown on our Fleet page. Please tell us about large items such as surfboards, bicycles or extra suitcases when booking so we can send a suitable vehicle.

8. Changes by us

Occasionally we may need to change your assigned chauffeur or vehicle. Any replacement will be of the same or a higher standard. If a significant change affects your booking, we will tell you as soon as possible and you may cancel for a full refund.

9. Insurance and liability

All our vehicles carry valid commercial passenger insurance as required by Sri Lankan law. We are not liable for delays, losses or costs caused by events outside our control, such as weather, road closures, traffic, strikes, flight changes or the actions of hotels and other suppliers. Our total liability for any booking is limited to the amount you have paid us for that booking, except where the law does not allow this limit.

10. Complaints

If you are unhappy with any part of the service, please tell your chauffeur or contact us on WhatsApp straight away so we can fix it during your trip. You can also write to bookings@ceylonchauffeur.com within 30 days of your tour ending.

11. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, and any dispute will be handled by the courts of Sri Lanka.

12. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The version shown on this page on the date you confirm your booking applies to that booking.
