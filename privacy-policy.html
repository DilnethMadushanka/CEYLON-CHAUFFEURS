Privacy Policy

We only collect the information we need to plan and run your trip. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Cancellation & Refund Terms & Conditions Privacy Policy

1. Information we collect

  • Booking details you give us: your name, email address, phone / WhatsApp number, country, travel dates, number of passengers, flight details, pickup and drop-off places, preferred route and any special requests.
  • Account details: if you create an account or sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo.
  • Messages: emails and WhatsApp messages you send us.
  • Basic technical data: pages visited, browser and device type and approximate location, used to understand how the website is used.

2. How we use it

  • To prepare your quotation, confirm and run your booking, and assign your chauffeur and vehicle.
  • To contact you about your booking by email, WhatsApp or phone.
  • To handle payments, refunds and any complaints.
  • To improve our website and services.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not send you marketing messages unless you ask us to.

3. Who we share it with

  • Your chauffeur, who receives your name, phone number, pickup details and itinerary.
  • Hotels, safari operators and other suppliers, only when we book something for you, and only the details they need.
  • Service providers who run our website and email: Google Firebase (secure storage of bookings and accounts), EmailJS (sending booking emails) and WhatsApp (messages). These providers process data on our behalf.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

4. Cookies and browser storage

The website stores a few small items in your browser (local storage) to make it work: your chosen language and currency, a draft of the booking form so you do not lose it, and your sign-in status. You can clear these at any time in your browser settings.

5. How long we keep it

We keep booking records for up to 5 years after your trip for accounting and legal reasons, and then delete them. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

6. Keeping your data safe

Booking data is stored with Google Firebase using secure, encrypted connections, and only authorised Ceylon Chauffeurs staff can access it.

7. Your rights

In line with Sri Lanka’s Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 (and the GDPR for travellers from Europe), you can ask us to:

  • show you the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your data, where we no longer need it for your booking or the law;
  • stop using your data for a particular purpose.

Email info@ceylonchauffeur.com and we will reply within 30 days.

8. Children

Bookings must be made by an adult. We only hold children’s details (such as age, for child seats) when a parent or guardian gives them to us as part of a booking.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
